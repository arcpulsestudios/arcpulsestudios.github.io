WageCue

A personal shift calendar and gross-pay checker for hourly and shift workers. Plan shifts, estimate gross pay, and compare it with your payslip, privately on your device.

By T Ganesh (Arc Pulse Studios)

Support

Questions, bugs, or purchase problems: email arcpulsestudios@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and the WageCue version from Settings → About → Version.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Free features work offline without signing in. Google sign-in is only needed to start or restore WageCue Pro.
  • Is the pay figure my official pay? No. WageCue estimates gross pay from the rules you enter. It does not calculate tax or deductions and is not a payroll service.
  • How do I restore Pro on a new phone? Open Settings → WageCue Pro → Restore purchases and sign in with the same Google account.
  • How do I cancel Pro? In the Google Play Store, open Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → WageCue.
