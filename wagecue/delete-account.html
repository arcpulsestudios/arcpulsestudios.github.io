Delete your WageCue account

WageCue by T Ganesh (Arc Pulse Studios). You do not need to reinstall the app to request deletion.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

Open WageCue and go to Settings → Account & sync → Delete account, then confirm. Your account and cloud data are deleted straight away.

Option 2: by email

Send an email to arcpulsestudios@gmail.com from the Google account you use with WageCue, with the subject Delete my WageCue account. We may ask you to confirm the request before deleting anything. We complete requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your WageCue account and its Google sign-in link
  • All jobs, pay rules, shifts, payslip checks, notes, and settings stored in WageCue cloud sync
  • Sessions and device records

What is kept, and for how long

  • A minimal purchase record (a one-way token hash, product, and dates) that is no longer linked to your account, kept to prevent purchase fraud and meet legal accounting duties
  • Encrypted server backups can contain your data for up to 30 days before they are rotated out

Things to know

  • Deleting your account does not cancel a WageCue Pro subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Data stored only on your phone is not affected. Remove it in WageCue with Settings → Delete all data on this device, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Files you exported or shared elsewhere must be deleted there.
