WageCue is provided by T Ganesh (Arc Pulse Studios) (we, us, or our). This Privacy Policy explains how the WageCue mobile application handles information.

1. Privacy Summary

WageCue's Free features work offline without creating an account. Your jobs, hourly rates, pay rules, shifts, payslip comparisons, notes, preferences, and generated reports are stored locally unless you export them or enable a Pro account feature.

On Android, starting or restoring WageCue Pro requires Google sign-in. Pro can back up and synchronize selected WageCue records through our server. We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or include a behavioral advertising SDK.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

WageCue may store the following information locally:

  • Workplace and optional role labels you enter
  • Currency, hourly rates, overtime rules, premiums, and allowances
  • Shift dates, times, breaks, status, and notes
  • Payslip gross amounts, comparison results, and resolution notes
  • Locale, time-zone, theme, reminder, and application preferences
  • Purchase entitlement cache needed to make paid features available
  • Account and synchronization status when Pro is enabled

Free users who do not sign in keep this information on their device unless they use a user-initiated export, backup, or sharing feature.

3. Information We Collect

When you use an optional Pro account, we may process:

  • Account information: the Google account identifier provided for sign-in, email address, and display name
  • Synchronized content: jobs, role labels, rates, pay rules, shifts, notes, adjustments, patterns, payslip values/notes, and relevant preferences that you choose to upload
  • Purchase information: marketplace, product identifier, purchase token or transaction identifier, purchase state, entitlement dates, and verification results; we do not receive your full payment-card number
  • Service and security information: random installation/device identifier, app/platform version, sync cursors and timestamps, IP address and request logs needed for delivery, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and security

We use this information to authenticate you, synchronize and restore your Pro data, verify and provide purchases, secure the service, respond to support, and comply with legal obligations. We do not include advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs. If diagnostics or analytics are added, this policy and the store disclosures must be updated before release.

4. Purchases

If you purchase WageCue Pro, Google Play processes the Android transaction under its own privacy policy. WageCue sends the purchase token and product identifier to our server, which verifies purchase state with Google Play and stores the entitlement information needed to provide and restore Pro. We do not receive your full payment-card information.

For marketplace privacy information, consult the privacy policy for Google Play or Apple's App Store in your region.

5. Notifications

If you enable shift reminders, WageCue requests notification permission and schedules notifications using operating-system services. Reminder content is derived from information stored on your device. You can disable reminders in WageCue or in system settings.

6. Exports, Backups, and Sharing

When you create a PDF, CSV, or backup, WageCue generates it locally. If you use the system share sheet, cloud drive, email application, messaging application, or another destination, that provider processes the file under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not control the destination you select.

If you enable Pro synchronization, the records listed in Section 3 are sent to our server over an encrypted connection and stored so they can be restored or synchronized on your signed-in devices. Turning off synchronization stops new uploads but does not by itself delete data already stored on the server. Use the account-deletion control to delete the account and its cloud data.

7. Permissions

WageCue may request only permissions needed for enabled features, including:

  • Notifications, when you choose to enable shift reminders
  • Run at startup and vibrate, so scheduled reminders survive a restart and can alert you
  • Background work and network state, so Pro synchronization can run when a network is available
  • Google Play billing, to offer and restore WageCue Pro
  • File or document access through system pickers when you export or restore a backup

Google sign-in and Google Play may open their own account or purchase surfaces. Network access is used for optional accounts, synchronization, purchase verification, policy/help pages, and service security.

The application should not request contacts, precise location, microphone, call-log, SMS, or broad media-library access for its planned functionality.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local records remain on your device until you delete individual records, use WageCue's delete-all-data control, remove synced records while signing out, clear application storage, or uninstall the application.

Cloud records remain while your account exists so signed-in users can restore them, including when a Pro subscription expires. You can request account and cloud-data deletion inside WageCue or at https://arcpulsestudios.github.io/wagecue/delete-account.html. After a valid request, we delete or de-identify account and synchronized content within 30 days, except information we must retain for fraud prevention, security, tax, accounting, dispute, or legal obligations. Backups may retain encrypted copies for up to 30 days before rotation.

Deleting a WageCue account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Subscription cancellation is managed through Google Play.

Files you exported or shared must be deleted from their destination separately. App marketplaces may retain purchase records according to their legal duties and privacy policies.

9. Security

We use HTTPS in transit, access controls, input validation, limited credentials, hashed session tokens, and protected backups. Sensitive mobile session material uses platform secure storage. No storage method is completely secure, and you are also responsible for protecting access to your device and exported files.

10. Children's Privacy

WageCue is intended for workers and is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum digital-consent age in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International Use

WageCue can display different currencies and regional formats. This does not mean WageCue provides payroll, tax, employment, or legal compliance for every country. Your records remain subject to the laws that apply to you.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when WageCue's functionality or legal duties change. We will update the effective date and provide notice where required.
