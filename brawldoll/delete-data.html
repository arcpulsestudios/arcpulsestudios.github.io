Brawldoll — Delete your data

Game: Brawldoll (com.arcpulsestudios.brawldoll)
Platforms: Android
Updated: 27 October 2026
Developer: Arc Pulse Studios

Your Brawldoll progress and profile are saved to the cloud under your Unity player ID, which is linked to your Google account or Google Play Games profile if you signed in with one. You can request deletion of your account and all its data.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the game, tap your profile in the top-left corner and note the PLAYER ID shown on the Profile page.
  2. Send an email to arcpulsestudios@gmail.com with the subject Brawldoll data deletion. Include your player ID and how you signed in (Google, Google Play Games or guest). If you signed in with Google, send the email from that Google address if you can.
  3. Never send passwords, verification codes or card details.
  4. We may ask for more information to find and verify the correct record. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days, subject to applicable law.

What is deleted

  • Your cloud save: nickname, profile picture and frame, outfits, level progress, coins, stats and purchase order IDs.
  • Your Unity player account and its links to your Google account or Google Play Games profile.
  • Analytics data associated with your player ID.

What is kept

  • Purchase records kept by Google Play under its own policies. Deleting game data does not cancel purchases or issue a refund.
  • Leaderboard scores and achievements stored by Google Play Games; you can remove them by deleting your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app.
  • Records we must keep for legal obligations, and limited correspondence needed to document your request.

You can also remove Brawldoll's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Uninstalling removes local app data but does not itself delete cloud data. After deletion, progress and coins cannot be recovered.

See also the Privacy Policy.
