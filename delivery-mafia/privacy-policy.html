Delivery Mafia — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026
Game: Delivery Mafia (com.arcpulsestudios.deliverymafia)
Platforms: Android, iOS, macOS and Windows
Developer: Arc Pulse Studios — arcpulsestudios@gmail.com

Delivery Mafia is an online multiplayer delivery race for up to 10 players. You can play as a guest or sign in with Google, Google Play Games or a Unity account (Google, Apple or email) so your progress and profile follow you to a new device. This policy explains what the game and the services it uses collect, why, and the choices you have.

Sign-in and cloud save

You choose how to play on the first screen:

  • Guest: an anonymous Unity Authentication player ID. No name or email is collected. Guest progress may be lost if you sign out or uninstall.
  • Continue with Google: Google shares your account ID, name, email address and profile photo link with the game. We use the ID to sign you in to Unity Authentication and the name as your default in-game name. We do not show your email to other players and do not use it for marketing.
  • Google Play Games: your Play Games player ID and display name are used to sign you in and as your default name.
  • Unity account (iOS, macOS, Windows): Unity's sign-in page opens in your browser and you sign in with Google, Apple or email. The game receives a Unity account ID, used only to sign you in to Unity Authentication. See Unity's privacy policy.

You stay signed in until you choose Sign out on the Profile page. Your progress and profile are stored in Unity Cloud Save under your Unity player ID: nickname (a random driver name until you choose one), profile picture and frame (chosen from the game's own pictures; you cannot upload photos), cars and unlocked items, level and XP, coins, stats and store transaction IDs (to prevent duplicate delivery). Settings such as audio stay on your device.

Online matches

Matches are played online through Unity Multiplayer Services. To find and join a match, your Unity player ID and in-game name are shared with the match room, and your in-game name, car, position and score are shown to the other players in that match. You can create a private room that only players with its code can join. Unity's relay servers process your IP address and network information to connect players; it is not shown to other players. The game has no text or voice chat.

Leaderboards and achievements

If you sign in to Google Play Games, scores and achievements may be sent to Google Play Games and shown with your Play Games name and avatar to other players. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising

Ads are always optional: you choose to watch a rewarded ad for a bonus. Ads are never forced after a match. Ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), which may use mediated ad networks. They may collect your device's advertising ID, device and network information, IP address (for approximate country or region) and how you interact with ads.

The first time you open the game you can choose whether ads may be personalized, and change this any time in Settings. If you decline, ads are not personalized. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (on iOS the game asks for tracking permission before using it). See the LevelPlay (ironSource) privacy policy and Unity's privacy policy.

Analytics and crash reports

With your consent (the same choice as personalized ads, changeable in Settings), the game sends gameplay events associated with a pseudonymous player identifier to Unity Analytics. We use this only to balance and improve the game. If you decline, no analytics events are sent.

If the game crashes, a crash report (stack trace, device model, OS and app version) may be sent to Unity Cloud Diagnostics and to Google Play (Android vitals) or Apple (crash reports you choose to share). Diagnostic reports may include technical identifiers.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store through Unity IAP. We do not receive your card or bank details. The game processes product IDs, purchase status and transaction IDs to deliver items, prevent duplicate delivery and restore owned items.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not ask for your phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not use analytics data or your email address for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Cloud save and account data are kept while you play and deleted on request. Analytics data is kept by Unity for up to 13 months. To delete your account and cloud save, follow our deletion instructions. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days, subject to applicable law. Uninstalling removes local app data but does not itself delete cloud data or store purchase records.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to the services listed above, which store it on their own secured infrastructure.

Children

Delivery Mafia is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy, for example when new features or platforms are added. Material changes will be shown by a new effective date on this page.
