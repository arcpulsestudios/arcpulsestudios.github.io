FreezeBox — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026
App: FreezeBox (com.arcpulsestudios.freeze_box)
Developer: Arc Pulse Studios

FreezeBox is a utility that lets you freeze (disable) and unfreeze apps on your own device without root. App-management data stays on your device; analytics, advertising and purchase verification involve the services described below. This policy includes the rewarded-ad release from version 1.0.16 and relevant records retained from older versions.

Information we do NOT collect

  • FreezeBox does not require an account or ask for your name or email to function. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to provide.
  • FreezeBox does not access contacts, messages, photos or precise GPS location. Advertising services may infer approximate location from your IP address.
  • The list of apps installed on your device is read on your device so you can choose what to freeze. This list is used only on the device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

Rewarded advertising and privacy choices

From version 1.0.16, free users can choose to watch a Google AdMob rewarded ad to activate a loadout. Activation is granted after the ad reports that the reward was earned. Premium activation is ad-free. Deactivating loadouts and manually unfreezing apps do not require ads.

Google Mobile Ads collects and shares information for ad delivery and measurement, analytics and fraud prevention, including:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
  • Device and other identifiers, including Android advertising ID and app set ID, where available.
  • Interactions such as ad views, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostics about app and SDK performance.

Data handling depends on privacy choices, device settings and service configuration. FreezeBox uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request privacy choices where required and checks whether ads may be requested. When available, use Settings > Ad privacy choices to revisit those choices. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings. Non-personalized or limited ads may still involve data processing. FreezeBox does not send your installed-app inventory or loadout contents to advertising services.

See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure. Premium removes ads, but does not disable analytics or purchase verification.

App usage analytics

From version 1.0.11, FreezeBox uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used so we can improve setup and features. It collects:

  • App interactions — for example which setup step you reached, that a freeze succeeded (a count only), that the Premium screen was opened, and whether a purchase was started or completed.
  • Device or other IDs — the Firebase app-instance ID, plus basic device information (model, Android version, country, app version).

Analytics never includes the names of the apps you freeze or the list of apps installed on your device, or your loadout contents. Firebase Analytics advertising-ID collection is disabled in FreezeBox. AdMob processing is separate from Firebase Analytics. Analytics data is processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms and kept for up to 14 months.

Information related to purchases

If you buy FreezeBox Premium, the payment is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. To confirm your purchase is genuine and active, the app sends the Google Play product ID and purchase token to our Supabase-hosted verification service. We store that token and your resulting entitlement status (whether Premium is active and, for subscriptions, its expiry) solely to grant you the features you paid for and to prevent fraud. We do not use it for any other purpose.

Older free-trial records

Version 1.0.16 removes the app-managed three-day Premium trial and no longer sends a device identifier to request that trial. Older versions may still send a hashed identifier derived from Android's per-app device ID to our service, which stores it with trial start and end dates to enforce one trial per device. Existing records have not automatically been deleted. They are used for legacy trial administration and fraud prevention, not advertising. You may request deletion as described below.

Permissions and why we use them

  • Query all packages — to list your installed apps so you can select which ones to freeze. This is the app’s core function; the list stays on your device.
  • Internet / network state — for the local wireless-ADB connection, purchase verification, catalog downloads, analytics, ads and privacy-choice requests.
  • Advertising ID — for Google Mobile Ads where available and permitted by device settings and applicable privacy choices.
  • Notifications — to guide the one-time Wireless Debugging pairing.
  • Billing — to offer Premium via Google Play.

Data storage and security

All data sent for purchase verification and free trials is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS. Purchase tokens, entitlement records and free-trial records are stored on our managed backend (Supabase) with access restricted to the service. App preferences and your loadouts are stored locally on your device. Google Mobile Ads encrypts its collected data in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

Google receives data for advertising, analytics and payments as described above; Supabase hosts our verification records. We do not sell app inventories or loadout data to data brokers. These providers may process data outside your country under their applicable privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Purchase/entitlement records are retained while your entitlement is active and for as long as needed for support and fraud prevention. To request deletion of your purchase-verification or legacy trial record, use the developer contact linked below. Legacy trial records are retained for trial administration and fraud prevention. Google handles advertising data under its own retention policies and applicable privacy choices.

You may also contact us to request access or correction; rights depend on your location. We may need information to identify your record. Do not send passwords or payment-card details. Deletion may affect Premium restoration until Google Play supplies the purchase again. Deleting records does not cancel a subscription; manage subscriptions in Google Play.

Clearing FreezeBox's storage removes local preferences and loadouts and resets the local analytics app-instance identity. It does not itself delete server-side records already collected. Use the ad privacy choices described above and Google's privacy controls for data Google handles.

Children

FreezeBox is a device-management tool intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes
