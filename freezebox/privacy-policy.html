FreezeBox — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026
App: FreezeBox (com.arcpulsestudios.freeze_box)
Developer: Arc Pulse Studios

FreezeBox is a utility that lets you freeze (disable) and unfreeze apps on your own device without root. We designed it to keep your data on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or any personal profile.
  • We do not use advertising, and we do not sell or share your data with data brokers.
  • The list of apps installed on your device is read on your device so you can choose what to freeze. This list is used only on the device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

App usage analytics

From version 1.0.11, FreezeBox uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used so we can improve setup and features. It collects:

  • App interactions — for example which setup step you reached, that a freeze succeeded (a count only), that the Premium screen was opened, and whether a purchase was started or completed.
  • Device or other IDs — the Firebase app-instance ID, plus basic device information (model, Android version, country, app version).

Analytics never includes the names of the apps you freeze or the list of apps installed on your device. We do not use analytics data for advertising, and it is not sold or shared with third parties for their own purposes. Data is processed by Google as our service provider under the Firebase privacy terms and kept for up to 14 months.

Information related to purchases

If you buy FreezeBox Premium, the payment is processed entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. To confirm your purchase is genuine and active, the app sends the Google Play purchase token for your transaction to our verification service. We store that token and your resulting entitlement status (whether Premium is active and, for subscriptions, its expiry) solely to grant you the features you paid for and to prevent fraud. We do not use it for any other purpose.

Permissions and why we use them

  • Query all packages — to list your installed apps so you can select which ones to freeze. This is the app’s core function; the list stays on your device.
  • Internet / network state — for the on-device wireless-ADB connection that performs freezing, to verify purchases with Google Play, and to send app usage analytics.
  • Notifications — to guide the one-time Wireless Debugging pairing.
  • Billing — to offer Premium via Google Play.

Data storage and security

All data sent for purchase verification is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS. Purchase tokens and entitlement records are stored on our managed backend (Supabase) with access restricted to the service. App preferences and your loadouts are stored locally on your device.

Data retention and deletion

Purchase/entitlement records are retained while your entitlement is active and for as long as needed for support and fraud prevention. To request deletion of your purchase-verification record, contact us at the address below. You can reset the analytics app-instance ID at any time by clearing FreezeBox's storage or reinstalling the app.

Children

FreezeBox is a device-management tool intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by a new effective date on this page.

Contact

Questions or data-deletion requests can be sent to the developer contact shown on FreezeBox's Google Play listing — Arc Pulse Studios.
