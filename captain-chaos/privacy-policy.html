Game: Captain Chaos (com.arcpulsestudios.captainchaos)

Captain Chaos is a single-player tow-surfing game. You do not create an account with us and we never ask for your name, email, contacts or precise location. This policy explains what the game and the services it uses collect, why, and the choices you have.

Cloud save and sign-in

When the game starts it signs in to Unity Authentication with an anonymous player ID and, if you use Google Play Games, links it to your Play Games account. Your game progress is stored in Unity Cloud Save so it can be restored after reinstalling. It contains only game data: level progress, stars, coins and gems, the daily chest streak, unlocked and equipped cosmetics, whether you bought Remove Ads or the starter pack, and the Google Play order IDs of your purchases (to prevent duplicate delivery). Audio settings stay on your device.

Leaderboards and achievements

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your scores (for example Endless distance, total stars, highest level) and achievements are sent to Google Play Games and shown with your Play Games name and avatar to other players. You can manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games app. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising

Ads are optional rewards (for example a second chance or double coins) plus an occasional interstitial ad between levels, never after a failed level, and never if you bought Remove Ads. Ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) which may use mediated ad networks. They may collect your device's advertising ID, device and network information, IP address (for approximate country or region) and how you interact with ads.

The first time you open the game you can choose whether ads may be personalized. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See the LevelPlay (ironSource) privacy policy and Unity's privacy policy.

Analytics and crash reports

With your consent (the same choice as personalized ads, changeable in Settings), the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Unity Analytics, such as which level you started, finished or failed and how far you went in Endless mode. We use this only to balance and improve the game. If you decline, no analytics events are sent.

If the game crashes or hits an error, a crash report (stack trace, device model, OS and app version) may be sent to Unity Cloud Diagnostics and to Google Play through Android vitals so we can fix it. Crash reports contain no personal profile information.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The game checks the purchase receipt on your device and stores the order ID in your cloud save so each purchase is delivered exactly once.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not use analytics data for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Cloud save data is kept while you play and deleted on request. Analytics data is kept by Unity for up to 13 months. To delete your cloud save and associated player ID, email arcpulsestudios@gmail.com from the email address you use with Google Play and include your Play Games name; we will delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to the services listed above, which store it on their own secured infrastructure.

Children

Captain Chaos is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be shown by a new effective date on this page.
