If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. This policy explains what the game and the services it uses collect, why, and the choices you have.

Cloud save and sign-in

When the game starts it signs in to Unity Authentication with an anonymous player ID and, on Android if you use Google Play Games, links it to your Play Games account. Your game progress is stored in Unity Cloud Save. Restoring progress requires access to the same Unity player identity. The current iOS release uses anonymous sign-in and does not link to Game Center; recovery after reinstalling or changing devices is not guaranteed. Android and iOS progress is not automatically shared. Cloud saves contain game data: level progress, stars, coins and gems, the daily chest streak, unlocked and equipped cosmetics, whether you bought Remove Ads or the starter pack, and the store transaction IDs of your purchases (to prevent duplicate delivery). Audio settings stay on your device.

Leaderboards and achievements

On Android, if you sign in to Google Play Games, your scores (for example Endless distance, total stars, highest level) and achievements are sent to Google Play Games and shown with your Play Games name and avatar to other players. You can manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games app. See the Google Privacy Policy.

The current iOS release does not provide Game Center leaderboards, achievements or account linking.

Advertising

Ads are optional rewards (for example a second chance or double coins) plus an occasional interstitial ad between levels, never after a failed level. Remove Ads and the starter pack remove interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads remain available. Ads are delivered by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) which may use mediated ad networks. They may collect your device's advertising ID, device and network information, IP address (for approximate country or region) and how you interact with ads.

The first time you open the game you can choose whether ads may be personalized. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See the LevelPlay (ironSource) privacy policy and Unity's privacy policy.

On iOS, the game requests App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before initializing ads when a permission request is needed. Permission is optional. Personalized-ad consent requires both your in-game choice and Apple's tracking authorization. If you deny tracking, the game does not authorize advertising tracking or access to IDFA; ad providers may still process information needed for contextual ads, delivery, fraud prevention and measurement that does not constitute tracking. You can change Apple's permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Refusing permission does not block gameplay.

Analytics and crash reports

With your consent (the same choice as personalized ads, changeable in Settings), the game sends gameplay events associated with a pseudonymous player identifier to Unity Analytics, such as which level you started, finished or failed and how far you went in Endless mode. We use this only to balance and improve the game. If you decline, no analytics events are sent.

If the game crashes or hits an error, a crash report (stack trace, device model, OS and app version) may be sent to Unity Cloud Diagnostics and to Google Play through Android vitals so we can fix it. On iOS, Apple may process crash and performance reports according to your system analytics-sharing settings. Diagnostic reports may include technical identifiers; they are not necessarily anonymous.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play on Android and the Apple App Store on iOS, through Unity IAP. We do not receive your card or bank details. The game processes product IDs, purchase status and transaction IDs to deliver rewards, prevent duplicate delivery and restore owned non-consumables.

On iOS, use Restore Purchases in the gem shop with the Apple Account that made the purchase to restore permanent benefits such as interstitial-ad removal and the starter pack's board. Consumable gem packs and one-time bonus gems are not replayed by entitlement restoration. See Apple's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • Gameplay does not request your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Support receives information you voluntarily send.
  • We do not use analytics data for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Cloud save data is kept while you play and deleted on request. Analytics data is kept by Unity for up to 13 months. To delete your cloud save and associated player ID, follow our Android and iOS deletion instructions. We may need information identifying the correct Unity player record; an Apple Account email alone does not identify an anonymous iOS cloud save. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days, subject to applicable law. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve requests and meet legal obligations. Uninstalling removes local app data, but does not itself delete cloud data or store purchase records.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to the services listed above, which store it on their own secured infrastructure.

Children

Captain Chaos is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.
