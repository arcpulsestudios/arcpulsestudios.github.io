Captain Chaos — Delete your data

Game: Captain Chaos (com.arcpulsestudios.captainchaos)
Developer: Arc Pulse Studios

Captain Chaos has no in-game accounts. Your progress is saved to the cloud under an anonymous player ID (linked to your Google Play Games profile if you sign in). You can ask us to delete it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to arcpulsestudios@gmail.com with the subject Captain Chaos data deletion.
  2. Include your Google Play Games player name (shown on the game's leaderboards) and, if possible, the email address of the Google account you play with.
  3. We confirm by email and delete your data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your cloud save: level progress, stars, coins, gems, daily chest streak, cosmetics and purchase order IDs.
  • Your Unity player ID and its link to Google Play Games.
  • Analytics data associated with your player ID.

What is kept

  • Records Google Play keeps for purchases (managed by Google, see your Google Play order history).
  • Leaderboard scores and achievements are stored by Google Play Games; you can remove them by deleting your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app.

You can also delete the data stored on your phone by uninstalling the game. Note that purchased gems and items are part of your save and cannot be restored after deletion.

See also the Privacy Policy.
