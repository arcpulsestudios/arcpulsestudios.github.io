Captain Chaos — Delete your data

Game: Captain Chaos (com.arcpulsestudios.captainchaos)
Platforms: Android and iOS
Updated: 3 October 2026
Developer: Arc Pulse Studios
iOS publisher: Omnexia Technologies Private Limited

Captain Chaos has no in-game accounts. Your progress is saved to the cloud under an anonymous Unity player ID (linked to your Google Play Games profile on Android if you sign in). The current iOS release uses an anonymous identity and does not link to Game Center. You can request deletion on either platform without a Google Play account.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to arcpulsestudios@gmail.com with the subject Captain Chaos data deletion.
  2. State whether you play on Android or iOS. Include your Unity player ID if you already have it. On Android, also include your Google Play Games player name if you use it.
  3. On iOS, no Google account is required. If you do not know your Unity player ID, contact support before uninstalling and we will help determine what identifying information is available. An Apple Account email or a screenshot of progress alone may not identify your anonymous cloud record. Never send passwords, verification codes, card details or full device logs.
  4. We may request additional information to locate and verify the correct record. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days, subject to applicable law.

What is deleted

  • Your cloud save: level progress, stars, coins, gems, daily chest streak, cosmetics and purchase order IDs.
  • Your Unity player account and any Android Google Play Games link associated with it.
  • Analytics data associated with your player ID.

What is kept

  • Purchase records retained by Google Play on Android or Apple on iOS under their own policies. Deleting game data does not cancel purchases or issue a refund.
  • On Android, leaderboard scores and achievements are stored by Google Play Games; you can remove them by deleting your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app.
  • Records we must retain for legal obligations, and limited correspondence needed to document your request.

Uninstalling removes local app data but does not itself delete cloud data. After cloud deletion, progress, currency balances and consumable gem rewards cannot be recovered from that save. Owned non-consumable benefits may still be restored through the relevant store; on iOS use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that made the purchase. This does not replay consumable packs or one-time bonus gems.
